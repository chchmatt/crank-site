How a job goes through

A job is a prompt in a queue. Nothing about it is hidden: every step leaves a branch, a report or a log you can read.

  1. 01 · QUEUE
    Write the job
    A prompt with a topic, an order, and "after" for what must finish first. Jobs that need a person are marked, so the runners skip them.
  2. 02 · BUILD
    A runner builds it
    Claude works on a branch of its own. Each topic has its own runner, so unrelated work goes ahead side by side.
  3. 03 · CHECK
    Your checks decide
    Your own checks (type check, lint and tests, for example) run before anything lands. Work that fails stays on its branch with a plain report of what went wrong.
  4. 04 · MERGE
    Merged, then the next
    A merge runner lands passing work on main, one merge at a time for each branch, and the queue moves on.

Made for walking away

▶ Topics side by side
Independent work runs in parallel. Work that depends on other work waits its turn.
✓ Nothing broken reaches main
Only work that passes the checks is merged. A conflict becomes a fix job instead of a mess.
❚❚ Hold, pause, end a task
Hold the whole queue or pause a topic, and the running job finishes cleanly first. End a task and its unfinished work is kept on its branch.
■ A status page for your phone
See every runner, every job and what needs you, without opening the computer.
⚑ It tells you when it needs a person
Everything that can go on without you does. What cannot waits, marked, with the reason, and Crank can alert your phone.
⎋ On your own computer
Runners run on your machine with your repository. The status page only reads progress.

Where it came from

Crank was built to ship Doodle Poker, a poker app, one prompt at a time. It was good enough that it became its own tool.

What it needs today

Everything is built on your own PC or server. You control it from crankbuild.com. It needs Windows (Mac is planned), git, Claude Code and Node 22 or later. Claude can walk you through setup.
