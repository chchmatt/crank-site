Questions people ask

Short answers, each pointing to the rule that explains it. These are examples for Crank's help chat as much as for readers: every answer comes from a rule in the other pages.

Branches and GitHub

Why did the CB-123 branch get deleted? Because CB-123's work was safely merged into its topic branch, so the branch had nothing left that isn't already in the topic. Crank deletes a job branch only when every change on it is in the topic, Crank made the branch, and nobody else has added to it. Otherwise it keeps the branch and writes why on the job. → Branches: when a job branch is deleted

Why is CB-123's branch still there? One of the safety checks didn't pass: some of its changes aren't in the topic yet, someone else made or pushed to the branch, or the job isn't finished. The reason is on the job and in the controller's log. → Branches

What is crank/jobs/CB-123 on GitHub? CB-123 was paused or stopped part way, so Crank saved its unfinished work there to keep it safe and let it carry on from any computer. It's deleted once the job is merged, under the same checks. → Branches: a paused job keeps its work

Will removing a topic delete its branch? No. Crank never deletes a topic branch or a base branch automatically. → Branches

Why is a topic Needs operator with "Nothing was forced"? GitHub and your computer both have new commits on that branch, so it can't move forward without throwing one side away. Crank never does that; a person decides. Other topics carry on. → Branches: never force

Did Crank pull main into my version-2 branch? No. A non-main base is made from main once and never updated from it automatically. → Merging: base branches

Jobs

Why is my job Waiting and not Queued? It waits for another job that isn't Built yet; the note names it ("after CB-12, paused" means that job is paused). → Jobs: what a job waits for

I dragged a job up and it slid back down. Why? It can't sit above a job it waits for, so it went to just after the last one; or you dropped it among the pinned Building and Needs operator jobs. → Jobs: order

Why can't I pause a Building job? A job being built can't be paused. Use End task (now) or End after (when it finishes). → Runners: ending runners

My job is Built. Why isn't it done? Built means finished and tested in its topic; it's done when the topic merges into its base and the job is Merged. → Jobs: states

What does "(split from CB-11)" mean? CB-11 finished its own work, but some steps had to wait (for example, until the topic reached main). Those steps became this new job. → Jobs: split jobs

Runners

I pressed End task. Why is the job Paused? So it doesn't start again by itself and the jobs that wait for it don't run. Resume it when you're ready; its work was saved. → Runners

My computer went offline. What happened to the job? It went back Queued at the front of its topic, with its work saved, and it resumes by itself once you're back online. → Runners: going offline

Why does the page say Lost contact? The controller missed 4 heartbeats (about 2 minutes): the computer may be off, asleep or offline. → Runners: going offline

Why has a topic got no runner? Its card always says why, for example all runners busy, paused, scheduled, a usage limit, or nothing left to build. → Topics

Why did a job stop with "it used more memory than its cap"? The runner went over the memory cap set for its controller, so its task was ended and the job Paused. Raise the cap in project settings, or resume it. → Runners: how many

Usage and schedules

Why did runners stop starting jobs? Your Claude usage reached the threshold you set. Crank checks every minute and starts again as soon as you're under it. → Usage

Can Crank see or share my Claude usage? Your computer reads it to decide whether to start jobs. No figure ever leaves your computer. → Usage

My topic's window closed. Was its job stopped? No. A window only controls when jobs start; a running job always finishes. → Schedules

Merging

Why did my topic merge while one of its jobs was still Paused? A topic merges when nothing is Building or Needs operator and at least one job is Built. Paused, scheduled and waiting jobs join a later merge. Only finished work is ever merged. → Merging

Why hasn't my topic merged? Check its merge rule (Not before, After topics, Hold) and its card: it may be testing against main, held for Release, waiting for a fix job, or have something Building or Needs operator. → Merging

Why did it merge without running the tests again? It was already fully tested against the current main, and main hadn't changed since. → Merging: tested against main

Can I make a topic merge right now? Coming soon: press Test and merge after current job. When the current job finishes, the topic pulls its base, runs the full tests, fixes what fails and merges, whatever its merge rule says. Other jobs in the topic wait meanwhile, so fixes land in one place. → Merging: on request

Can I run the full tests without merging? Coming soon: Test after current job, or the automatic setting (every N jobs or every N hours). → Merging: on request

Controllers and privacy

What does Crank send from my computer? The queue's state, plain reasons, the heartbeat time and your commands. Never logs, memory or usage figures, or your computer's name, specs, paths or IP. → Privacy

Another computer says "Run by Controller 1". Why won't it start? Only one controller runs a project at a time. If Controller 1 has gone quiet, press Take over. → The lease

Will another computer take over if mine dies? Only when you press Take over. Nothing takes over by itself. → Taking over
