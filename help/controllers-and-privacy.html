Controllers, the API and your privacy

The controller is the program on your computer that runs a project. This page covers how it's identified, why only one runs a project at a time, how it talks to Crank, and what never leaves your computer.

Coming later: accounts and sign-in

Planned, not yet built:

  1. No passwords, ever. Sign in with an emailed one-time code, Google or Microsoft. GitHub later.
  2. Personal details (email, name) are stored encrypted with a key of your own. When you ask to be forgotten, that key is thrown away, so your details can never be read again. Forgetting completes within 7 days (when the last backup of the keys expires).

Source: event sourcing design, sections 9.4, 9.8, 9.9.
