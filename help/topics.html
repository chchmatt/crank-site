Topics

A topic is a line of work in a project, such as page, api or runners. Every job belongs to one topic.

Why topics exist

  1. Each topic has its own runner and its own branch, so different topics build at the same time without stepping on each other. Why: one queue in a single line is slow; jobs in different areas rarely touch the same files.
  2. Within a topic, jobs build one at a time, in list order. Why: jobs in the same area often change the same files, and one at a time means each starts from the last one's finished work.
  3. Each topic merges on its own, when it's ready and its merge rule allows (see Merging).

Topic settings (in project settings) Coming soon (CB-50, CB-71, CB-74, CB-76, CB-80)

Each topic has its own section in project settings:

  1. Pause, with an optional one-line reason (up to 140 characters). No new job in the topic starts. A job already building finishes first.
  2. Restart. The topic runs again. Jobs that were paused on their own stay paused. Why: you paused those for a reason of their own; restarting the topic shouldn't undo that.
  3. Remove. Only when the topic has no open jobs (none Waiting, Queued, Paused, Building or Needs operator). Finished jobs keep the topic's name, and the done list still shows it. Removing a topic never deletes its branch (see Branches).
  4. Runs: when its jobs may start (see Usage and schedules).
  5. Merge: when it merges, and whether it keeps up to date while waiting (see Merging).
  6. Base branch: the branch it starts from and merges into, main by default.
  7. Squash merges: use the project setting, on, or off.

A paused topic's card says "Paused: <your reason>", or "Paused by the operator" with no reason.

Source: CB-50, CB-71, CB-74, CB-76, CB-80.

A topic with no runner always says why Coming soon (CB-40, CB-61, CB-62, CB-67, CB-70)

When a topic has open jobs but no runner, its card always shows one short line saying why. Never blank, never only an icon. For example:

  • "starting a runner…"
  • "all 5 runners busy"
  • "waiting for CB-31"
  • "CB-12: the runner would not build it: <reason>"
  • "merging to main" or "waiting to merge (3rd in line)"
  • "merge blocked, fix job CB-29"
  • "paused by the operator" or "Paused: <reason>"
  • "Hold all is on"
  • "Scheduled: overnight 22:00 to 07:00, next start 22:00"
  • "Waiting: Claude usage limit, resumes 15:45"
  • "Waiting for free memory"
  • "Runners ended after their jobs by the operator"
  • "nothing left to build"

Why: the owner asked "why hasn't the queue topic got a runner?" and the card didn't say. An empty card looks like a fault.

Source: CB-40, CB-61, CB-62, CB-67, CB-70.

Coming later: topics you define

Planned, not yet built: you define the project's topics in settings, each with a name and a one-line description. A new job with no topic is put in the best fit by AI (marked "auto"), or in general if nothing fits. You can change a job's topic until it starts building; after that it's locked, because its work is already on that topic's branch. A topic can be retired only when it has no open jobs.

Source: event sourcing design, section 2.5.
