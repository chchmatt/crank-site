The status page

The status page at crankbuild.com shows your project's queue and carries your commands to your computer. Its title is your project's name, with a settings cog and a profile icon at the right.

Live updates

The page updates within a few seconds of a change. If the live connection drops, it falls back to checking every so often and reconnects by itself. A phone in your pocket holds no connection.

Source: CB-9 to CB-11.

Lost contact

If the controller misses 4 heartbeats (about 2 minutes), the page says "Lost contact with the controller since HH:MM", and Building jobs show "Building (last heard HH:MM)". See Runners.

Source: CB-44.

Alerts

Crank sends phone alerts only when something needs you, for example "CB-4: Needs operator: <what it waits for>". At most one alert per thing every 10 minutes. No pop-ups on your computer, except the desktop app's notification when something needs you.

Source: CB-12, CB-22, CB-46.
