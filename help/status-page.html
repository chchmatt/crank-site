The status page

The status page at crankbuild.com shows your project's queue and carries your commands to your computer. Its title is your project's name, with a settings cog and a profile icon at the right.

The list Coming soon (CB-20, CB-32, CB-38, CB-57)

  1. Each row shows one state, with its own symbol and colour, and the same words everywhere: Waiting, Queued, Paused, Building, Needs operator, Built, Merged, Cancelled. State is never shown by colour alone.
  2. State chips above the list filter it, in any mix: To do (Waiting, Queued, Paused), Active (Building, Needs operator, Built), Done (Merged), Cancelled. The default is To do plus Active. Your choice is remembered in your browser only. Topic chips filter too, together with the state chips.
  3. Needs operator can't be hidden. The Active chip carries a red badge with the number of jobs that need you, even when Active is off. Pressing it turns Active on.
  4. Needs operator and Building jobs are pinned at the top (see Jobs).
  5. Built rows say what they wait for: "waiting for topic X to reach main", or the topic's merge note.
  6. Merged rows show their topic and when they merged.
  7. Long notes are cut to two lines and never squash the rest of the row; the full note is on the job's page. Nothing scrolls sideways on a phone.
  8. Buttons show at once that they were pressed, then the controller's answer.

Source: CB-20, CB-32, CB-38, CB-57.

Estimates Coming soon (CB-17, CB-58)

  1. Each open job shows "~N min", with one line of why on hover or tap ("like CB-4 and CB-6: one page file and its tests").
  2. A job being built shows "N min so far" against its estimate. A merged job shows "estimate about N min, built in M min".
  3. The done view shows how close estimates have been, for example "estimates within half: 7 of 10".
  4. Estimates are made by AI from your own project's finished jobs (same topic or kind, similar files, nearby size). With fewer than 5 finished jobs, it says "no history yet" and uses the size's default. They cover the builder's own time, not the final tests and merge.

Source: CB-17, CB-58.

Live updates

The page updates within a few seconds of a change. If the live connection drops, it falls back to checking every so often and reconnects by itself. A phone in your pocket holds no connection.

Source: CB-9 to CB-11.

Lost contact Coming soon (CB-44)

If the controller misses 4 heartbeats (about 2 minutes), the page says "Lost contact with the controller since HH:MM", and Building jobs show "Building (last heard HH:MM)". See Runners.

Source: CB-44.

Project settings Coming soon (CB-27, CB-50, CB-63, CB-67, CB-71, CB-74, CB-76, CB-80)

The settings cog opens project settings:

  • Job prefix (for example CB) and number of runners.
  • Controllers: each controller's name and limits.
  • Topics: pause, restart, remove, Runs window, Merge rule, Keep up to date, Base branch, Squash.
  • Branches: each non-main base branch's merge lane (starts immediately, or uses the runner pool).
  • Squash merges for the project.
  • Claude usage threshold.

Source: CB-27, CB-50, CB-63, CB-67, CB-71, CB-74, CB-76, CB-80.

Alerts Coming soon (CB-22, CB-46)

Crank sends phone alerts only when something needs you, for example "CB-4: Needs operator: <what it waits for>". At most one alert per thing every 10 minutes. No pop-ups on your computer, except the desktop app's notification when something needs you.

Source: CB-12, CB-22, CB-46.
